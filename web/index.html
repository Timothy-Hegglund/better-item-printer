    How to use the tracker

    Set your console clock to match the tracked console time, or enter its difference from the PC in seconds. Live forecasts show the result of opening the printer at that second. The game seeds its RNG when you first enter the printer; it does not keep reseeding while you stay inside.

    To hit a target, open the printer at the target console time. Account for your controller and game delay. Use session mode for additional jobs without leaving the printer. This tool calculates results; it does not read or control your Switch.
